01Introduction

“Where are you?”

Hyma Naga Jyothi

I spent years waiting for someone to come. These are the lessons I learned when I stopped waiting.

Born in Kakinada. Living in Hyderabad. Founder of Parishva Branding House and Parishva NPO.

This is not a resume. These are lessons I did not learn from a book. I learned them by living them.

Walk with me

02Childhood

You can learn to hold yourself.

Some of us grow up without the warmth we need. I learned not to wait my whole life for it to arrive, and to give it to myself first.

The comfort you give yourself is not a lesser kind. It is where every other kind begins.

Hold yourself first. Then you can hold others.

03Growing up

Silence is not safety.

I learned early to stay quiet so that things would look right from outside. It took me years to see that silence protects the appearance, never the person.

A voice that is not used does not disappear. It waits. Mine waited a long time.

How it looks is never more important than what is true.

04Finding my way

Love has to start with you.

For a long time, I looked for love anywhere it seemed to be offered, and I paid for it every time.

What I was really looking for was to be seen. No one could give me that until I had learned to see myself.

The love you are searching for outside begins inside.

05Marriage and motherhood

Trust your instinct.

A mother knows. When something feels wrong, it usually is, even when everyone tells you that you are overthinking.

On Love

I also learned that people can love you and still not know how to show it. Love shown badly is still love, and understanding that freed me from years of anger.

On Healing

And I learned that pain which is not healed gets passed on. It only stops with the one who chooses to heal.

Heal, so that it ends with you.

06Standing on my own

Your own ground gives you your voice.

A woman who depends on others for everything is rarely heard. Earning my own income changed how the world listened to me, and how I listened to myself.

I had no business degree, so I learned by doing:

HR First Business Makeup Video Production Events Teaching Founders Team Leadership

Each one taught me to see what is really there before acting.

Start where you are, with what you have.

07The Question

The question that changed my life was the simplest one.

“How are you?”

When someone asked me and meant it, I finally stopped and looked at myself. That is where healing began.

So now I ask it of everyone. And I ask it of myself.

08Standing up

No one is coming. Go anyway.

I spent years waiting for someone to fight for me. The day I understood that I had to do it myself was the day I became free.

If something is wrong, say it. Silence is how wrong things keep happening, to you and to the women who come after you.

If you do not stand up for yourself, no one will.

09Returning to myself

Surrender is not giving up.

Sacred Wisdom

The Bhagavad Gita taught me to act fully and hold the results lightly. Draupadi and Sati taught me what it means to endure and still stand.

Inner Awakening

I stopped asking to be saved, and started asking to be set free. I was never alone. I had been looking for Shiva all along.

Origins of Parishva

Parishva was born from that, named for the union of Parvathi and Shiva. When something I built was not right, I stopped it rather than pretend.

A setback is not proof you are not meant for this. It is how you are shaped.

10Today

Who I am now.

I am Hyma Naga Jyothi. Some call me Zara, a few call me Akkasoto, and the people closest to me call me Jo. I am a mother of two daughters, a founder, and a student of the Gita.

Each morning begins with yoga. Each evening ends with Bharatanatyam, where I come most alive. In February 2027, I will perform at a Shivaratri I am hosting.

I built Parishva Branding House to help founders see what they cannot see about their own business. Clarity is what I had to find for myself, so it is what I give.

I am writing a memoir. Part one is complete. Part two is still being lived.
